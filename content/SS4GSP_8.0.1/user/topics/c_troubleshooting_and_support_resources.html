General product troubleshooting

Edit online

There are a number of self-help information resources and tools to help you troubleshoot problems.

  • Troubleshooting process overview
    Troubleshooting is the process of finding and eliminating the cause of a problem. When you have a problem with IBM DevOps Deploy (Deploy), the troubleshooting process begins as soon as you ask yourself what happened.
  • General information to collect
    Different issues require different set of information to diagnose and solve problems. However, there is a set of general information used to diagnosis all issues.
  • Searching online knowledge bases
    If you have a problem with IBM DevOps Deploy (Deploy), search the available knowledge bases to determine whether your problem is known, has a workaround, or if it is already resolved and documented.
  • Contacting IBM Software support
    If the self-help resources do not provide a resolution to your problem, you can contact IBM® Software support. IBM® Software support assists in resolving product issues.
